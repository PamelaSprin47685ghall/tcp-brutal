Known limits:
  - No NACK and no FEC retransmit: a block that loses more shares than its
    parity can recover stalls the stream until the close timer resets it.
  - FEC_FBK is accepted but does not drive an M adaptation in T2.
  - 256-carrier live QEMU is not run (slirp); T5.2 runs 256 on the real WAN.
  - The grouped block codec uses w=2n, d=w/2 (single group keeps w=n, d=n/2).
