In all three runs every c9 counter other than ack_tx/ack_rx/credit_wait is 0,
tx_ack reaches the full transfer size on both tunnels, and no probe reported a
new WARNING or UBSAN.

ACK cadence: one ACK per delivered super-block, 128 packets = 1 MiB at this
shape. 8 MiB -> 8 ACKs, 16 MiB -> 16, 32 MiB -> 32, in both directions.

Why 8 MiB proves nothing about credit: the sender's initial window is
slots*groups*n = 1024 packets = 8 MiB, so an 8 MiB stream fits in one window
and needs no ACK. 16 MiB cannot finish without the ACK refilling the window;
fec_ack_rx=16 and tx_ack=16 MiB on both sides show it did. credit_wait stays 0
because the ACK for a super-block arrives long before the sender can spend the
whole 8 MiB window on a loopback carrier set.

Baseline note: unmodified 42ed56f also crossed 16 MiB in this QEMU shape
(350 ms, 45.7 MiB/s each way, c9 clean, 0 warnings). The ticket's suspected
16 MiB stall did not reproduce here. The branch fixes the semantics that were
wrong (zero credit read as open, ACK pinned to the lowest carrier, frontier
allowed to move backwards, tail flushed past the window) and makes ACK cadence
and window stalls observable; it does not claim a QEMU throughput change.
